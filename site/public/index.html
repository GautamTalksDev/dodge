Transparency

Who tells everyone where they are.

Operators that publish their own orbit predictions let everyone screen against accurate positions instead of radar estimates. Some share privately with US Space Command, TraCSS or SpaceX instead. Only public sharing shows here.

Why this exists

Space is getting crowded, but the record of who crowds it should not be private. Every number here can be checked.

Open data

Check our work.

The screen runs every day on GitHub, from public data, with code anyone can read and rerun. Orbit recordings and every predicted pass are published as daily releases.
