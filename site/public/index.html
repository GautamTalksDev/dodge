Who is on the other side

Raw counts mostly follow how much hardware sits at Starlink's altitude. The per object column shows how often each object comes close.

Who tells everyone where they are

Operators that publish their own orbit predictions publicly, through CelesTrak's supplemental data, let everyone else screen against accurate positions instead of radar estimates. Some operators share privately with US Space Command or TraCSS instead; that does not show here.

Open by default

Every number on this page is rebuilt each day from public data, by code anyone can read and rerun. Read the method, download the data, or check our work.

Orbit data: CelesTrak. Starlink positions come from SpaceX's own published ephemerides (CelesTrak SupGP). DODGE does not use Space-Track data.
