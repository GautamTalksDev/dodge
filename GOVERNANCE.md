# Governance

DODGE is maintained by one person, [Gautam Khosla](https://gautamkhosla.com) (GitHub: @GautamTalksDev).

## Decisions

* **Method changes** (anything that can move a published number) are proposed in an issue or discussion first, with a validation run, and recorded in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) with the date they take effect.
* **Corrections** to published data are fixed forward: the next screen uses the fix, the changelog says what changed and why, and past releases are never edited. They are sealed and signed, so editing them would break the chain on purpose.
* **Everything else** goes through pull requests reviewed by the maintainer.

## Independence

DODGE is independent. It is not funded by, affiliated with or reviewed by SpaceX, Starlink, any satellite operator or any government. If that ever changes, it will be stated on the website and in this file.

## Continuity

All code, data and history are public, under open licences. If the maintainer stops maintaining DODGE, anyone can fork it and keep it running: the workflows need no secrets. The maintainer will archive the repository with a note rather than delete it.
